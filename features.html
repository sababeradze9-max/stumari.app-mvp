Digital Guest Guide

All the information in one place. Mobile-first, elegant, and instantly accessible without logging in.

Check-in & Check-out

Simple arrival and departure instructions. Keypad door codes, lockbox backup, and parking directions.

Wi-Fi

Easy connection for your guests. One tap copies the network name and password to their phone clipboard.

House Rules

Set clear expectations from the start. Quiet hours, smoking policy, pets, and care for historic spaces.

How Things Work

Appliance guides and useful info. Remote controls, espresso machines, hot water, and washing machine settings.

Local Recommendations

Restaurants, attractions and more. Curated neighborhood gems with walk times and direct Google Maps navigation.

Transport

Airport, taxi and public transport. Bolt & local taxi guides, metro cards, and estimated travel times.

Multilingual

Available in multiple languages. English, Georgian, and regional translations so every guest feels welcomed.

QR Code

Quick and easy access. Automatically generated, ready-to-print 5x7 or A4 stands for kitchen counters and bedside tables.

NFC

Tap to open your guide. Program low-cost NFC stickers or wood tags on the front door so guests tap and enter.

Offline Reception-Proof

Built-in Service Worker caches the guide automatically. Guests can retrieve keypad codes and Wi-Fi even in elevators or remote mountain stays.

Direct Host Assistance

Instant phone dialer and WhatsApp chat. Guests reach you in one tap without digging through Airbnb or booking apps.
